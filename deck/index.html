why.

The sustainable fare costs more, and next to a cheaper fare it usually loses. A price gap alone will not change that, so the fare needs a reward you can see and keep.

The timing is right. Air India and Airbus announced India's first voluntary corporate SAF programme on 17 November 2026. India plans a 1% SAF blending mandate from 2027. This is the passenger version of that shift.

grow.

Book the sustainable fare and a tree appears on your boarding pass. Paper gets a QR code. Mobile gets a "Plant this tree" button. It unlocks when you land. A cancelled trip never grows a tree.

One tree stands for one booking's SAF. It is a symbol, not a planted sapling, and the receipt says so. That keeps the reward honest while giving the extra cost something to show for itself.

gather.

The species comes from where you flew. Banyan in Mumbai, cherry blossom in Tokyo. Rarity comes from occasions you earn, a first sustainable flight, Diwali, your tenth flight, a new route. Nothing is random.

Size and apple count follow the SAF you actually bought. Longer flight, bigger tree, more apples. Shake once and every apple falls. Then the tree becomes a collection piece. Empty plots invite the next booking, so a small garden is fine.

honest.

The fare buys a fixed extra quantity of SAF, used somewhere on Air India's network within about six months. Not necessarily in your plane. Lufthansa lost in court over that claim and renamed Green Fares to Impact Fares. The receipt says litres, feedstock, the date used and a certificate reference. It never says your plane.

Rarity never changes the impact claim. The headline is an everyday comparison: your garden kept 140 kg of CO2 out of the air, about the same as not driving a car from Delhi to Varanasi. One small line under it gives your share: your trees cut 10% of the CO2 from those flights. It never calls a flight carbon neutral. (Numbers are illustrative.)

again.

Apples are only earned on sustainable fares. One apple converts to 10 Maharaja Points, or takes 20 rupees off your next sustainable fare. They also buy extra baggage or a meal upgrade.

Money off the next sustainable fare is the headline perk. It closes the loop, so the reward leads back to the choice we wanted in the first place. Next is the clickable prototype, which walks the flow: booking, boarding pass, landing, garden, receipt, spend.
